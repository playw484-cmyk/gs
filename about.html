About Grocery Select

Grocery Select is a premium grocery retail brand developed to bring an organized and modern shopping experience to Indian consumers. The brand is owned and operated by Krisomika Retail Private Limited, a retail and distribution company focused on building a national network of modern grocery stores.

Vision

To build a Pan-India premium grocery retail chain that delivers quality products and a superior shopping experience.

Mission

  • Deliver high quality grocery products
  • Build organized retail infrastructure
  • Support entrepreneurs through franchise partnerships
  • Develop private label FMCG brands
