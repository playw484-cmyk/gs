Recruitment Process

Our hiring process is designed to be fast, fair, and candidate-friendly.

1

Application Review

The HR team reviews your profile, education, experience, and submitted resume against role requirements.

2

Screening Call

Shortlisted candidates are contacted for a brief conversation about availability, role fit, and salary expectations.

3

Interview & Assessment

Depending on the role, candidates may complete an interview round, operational assessment, or store-facing scenario evaluation.

4

Final Selection

Selected applicants receive the final communication with joining steps, documentation, and onboarding guidance.
